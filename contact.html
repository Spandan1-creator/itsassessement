Frequently Asked

Quick Answers

Do I need experience to trek?

Many of our treks are suitable for fit beginners. Langtang Valley and Annapurna Base Camp are great starting points. We always advise a good level of general fitness and some uphill walking practice before arrival.

What is the best time to visit Nepal?

October–November and March–April are the best seasons for trekking — clear skies, stable temperatures, and spectacular mountain views. Monsoon (June–September) is best avoided for high-altitude routes.

Do I need travel insurance?

Yes — travel insurance covering emergency helicopter evacuation is mandatory for all treks above 3,000 m. We can recommend reputable providers if needed.

How do I pay for my trek?

We accept bank transfer, major credit cards, and cash in NPR or USD. A 25% deposit confirms your booking; the balance is due 30 days before departure.
