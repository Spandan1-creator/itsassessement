About Himalayan Horizons

A Nepalese family business with a passion for sharing our mountains with the world.

Est. 2005 · Kathmandu, Nepal

Born in the Mountains

Himalayan Horizons was founded in 2005 by Pemba Sherpa and his family in Thamel, Kathmandu. After two decades guiding international climbers on Everest and the Annapurna ranges, Pemba wanted to create a company that went beyond logistics — one that shared the true soul of Nepal with every visitor.

Today we are a team of 35 certified guides, trekking staff, and travel coordinators, all born and raised in the communities we take you through. Our profits support local schools, trail maintenance, and reforestation projects across the Khumbu valley.

We are proud members of the Trekking Agents Association of Nepal (TAAN) and fully licensed by the Nepal Tourism Board.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🧭

Expert Local Knowledge

Our guides have grown up on these trails. They speak the local languages, know the mountain weather, and carry deep respect for the land they guide you through.

🌱

Responsible Tourism

We follow strict Leave No Trace policies, pay all our staff fair wages above national minimum, and donate 5% of profits to Himalayan conservation projects each year.

🤝

Community First

We hire locally, eat locally, and shop locally. Every night you spend in a teahouse on our treks goes directly into the pockets of the family who owns it.
